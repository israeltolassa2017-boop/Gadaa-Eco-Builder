About the Project

Gadaa Eco Builder is a growing initiative connecting Oromo indigenous knowledge, ecological restoration, climate action, education, and interactive gaming.

I am developing the project as an independent initiative and building its foundation step by step.

What I Am Looking For

I am looking for people and organizations who can help open the next door for Gadaa Eco Builder, including:

  • Fellowships and grant opportunities
  • Training and accelerator programs
  • Scholarships and learning opportunities
  • Vacancies, paid roles, consultancies, or project opportunities
  • Introductions to organizations, funders, mentors, or partners
  • Invitations to relevant programs, events, exchanges, or opportunities abroad
  • Small early funding, sponsorship, or other practical support

I am not only looking for direct funding. A recommendation, introduction, training opportunity, or invitation to the right program could also become an important next step.

Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision is to connect the digital Gadaa Eco Builder experience with real-world ecological restoration, cultural learning, community participation, and future physical destinations.

Ideas such as Odaa-inspired destinations and Gadaa Eco Builder Valley are part of the long-term direction. The immediate focus is on developing the core project, proving what works, and growing step by step.
